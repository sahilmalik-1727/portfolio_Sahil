HERO BACKGROUND VIDEO
=====================

The home page hero can play a looping background video. You have two options:

OPTION A — YouTube (this is the current default)
------------------------------------------------
Open index.html, find the block near the top that looks like:

    <div class="hero-video" id="hero-video" aria-hidden="true"
         data-yt="ZdP7jWkDmus"
         data-video="assets/herovideo.mp4">

Change data-yt="ZdP7jWkDmus" to your own YouTube video ID (the part after
watch?v= in a YouTube URL). The video auto-plays, muted, and loops forever.
(Browsers require background video to be muted — that's normal.)

OPTION B — Your own MP4 file (cleanest, no YouTube branding)
------------------------------------------------------------
Drop a video file into THIS folder named exactly:

    herovideo.mp4

That's it. If that file exists, the site uses it automatically instead of
YouTube — and a self-hosted MP4 loops perfectly with zero branding or flicker.

Tips for a good background loop:
  • Keep it short (10–30s) and let it loop.
  • 1920×1080, H.264 .mp4, ideally under ~10 MB so it loads fast.
  • It will be muted and cover the whole hero, with a soft tint over it so the
    headline stays readable.

To turn the video OFF entirely: just delete both data-yt and data-video values
(leave them empty), and the hero shows its warm gradient instead.
