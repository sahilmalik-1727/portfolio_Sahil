The story

I move across the edit bay, the design board and the camera — so an idea never loses its shape.

Most creatives pick one lane. I found early on that the most interesting work lives where they overlap: an edit paced like a story, a poster that thinks like a UI, a site built by someone who actually shoots the photos on it.

That range is the point. It means fewer hand-offs, tighter feedback loops, and work that feels intentional at every layer — because the same person cared about the color grade, the type, and the timing.
